# EN 301 549

Generation target: **WCAG 2.2 AA** plus applicable EN 301 549 requirements. This guidance does not certify EN conformity.

## Applicability triggers

Load only the clauses matching the product capability:

| Product capability | Evaluate |
|---|---|
| Web pages and web flows | Clause 9 plus WCAG conformance requirements |
| Accessibility-feature activation or information conversion | Applicable clause 5 requirements |
| Voice/video communication, real-time text, or total conversation | Clause 6 |
| Audio/video playback, transmission, conversion, or recording | Clause 7 and `references/topics/images-and-media.md` |
| Documents or forms downloadable from a web page, including PDF and Office files | Clause 10 and a document-accessibility workflow |
| Non-web software | Applicable clause 11 software requirements |
| Web-based authoring tool, CMS, editor, or page builder | Authoring-tool requirements: v3.2.1 clauses 11.8.2–11.8.5; v4.1.0 clauses 5.10.2–5.10.5 |
| User documentation, accessibility information, or support services | Clause 12 |
| Relay or emergency communication services | Clause 13 |
| Electronic programme guides or media discovery | Applicable v4.1.0 programme-guide requirements |

Do not load niche clauses for an ordinary content page. Re-evaluate when a feature adds one of these capabilities.

## Clause 9 (web)

EN 301 549 maps web content to WCAG and adds EN-specific context. Harmonised **v3.2.1** remains the legal baseline as of September 2026 and references WCAG 2.1 AA. Draft **v4.1.0**, aligned with WCAG 2.2, was adopted for publication on 24 August 2026 but is not harmonised until formally cited.

Targeting WCAG 2.2 AA prepares web UI for both WCAG baselines, but WCAG alone does not establish full EN 301 549 conformity. Evaluate applicable product, documentation, support, media, and ICT requirements separately. Do not treat WCAG AAA or WCAG 3 drafts as required here.

## Clause 7 (video)

For video and multimedia, load `references/topics/images-and-media.md`. Evaluate playback, synchronization, preservation, presentation, and activation of captions, spoken subtitles, and audio description—not only whether those alternatives exist.

## Clause 10 (documents)

Clause 10 can apply to documents and forms downloadable from web pages and to documents supplied as product documentation. Route PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and similar files to an appropriate document-accessibility workflow; web guidance alone is insufficient.

## Clause 12 (documentation and support)

User-facing help, instructions, and support content must themselves be accessible. Load structure, images/media, links, and cognitive topics. Keep help in a consistent relative location and do not ship critical instructions only as an inaccessible document.

Where applicable, product documentation identifies accessibility and compatibility features, explains how to activate and use them, and states known assistive-technology interoperability. Support services provide this accessibility information and communication methods that meet users’ needs.

## Conditional product scope

Closed functionality, biometrics, two-way voice, hardware, non-web documents/software, authoring tools, and communication services need separate evaluation only when the product provides those capabilities.

## EAA

The European Accessibility Act uses harmonised standards to provide a presumption of conformity. Treat this package as implementation guidance and confirm the currently cited standard and product scope before making a legal claim.
